# Natural Numbers
Natural Numbers are the set of positive integers used for counting and ordering. They
are the numbers we naturally use in everyday life.

Example: 1,2,3,4,5...

Note: Some definitions include 0 as a natural number, especially in modern mathematics and
computer science.

## Key Properties
Whole Number: No fractions or decimals

Countable: Can be listed one by one

Infinite: There is no largest natural number.

Closed under addition and multiplication: Adding or multiplying natural numbers always
produces another natural number.

Not closed under subtraction: Subtracting a larger number from a smaller one gives a
negative number, which is not a natural number.

## Uses
- Counting objects
- Ordering/Ranking
- Basis for more advanced number sets
